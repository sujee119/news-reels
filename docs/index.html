Reel Uploader

A personal tool that publishes short news videos.

Reel Uploader is a private automation project run by its author. It turns published news and research articles into short vertical videos and uploads them to the author's own social accounts, including the author's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Reads public RSS feeds and news articles.
  • Writes a short plain-language script and renders a vertical video.
  • Uploads that video to accounts owned by the author.

Who can use it

Nobody else. It is not a product or a service, has no users besides its author, and no sign-up exists. It authenticates only to the author's own accounts.
